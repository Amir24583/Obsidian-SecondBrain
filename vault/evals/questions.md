# Eval questions

Questions you want this vault to answer, each with the source that holds the
answer. `python .brain/sb.py eval` asks the wiki each one and checks the answer
cites that source. Aim for 20 to 30, across your topics.

Format, one per line:
`- Q: <question> | expect: <part of the source's path>`
